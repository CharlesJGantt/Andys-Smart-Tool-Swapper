# Andy's Smart Tool Swapper 0.2.3

Initial release. Start breaking a block and the right tool comes to hand — then your previous item comes straight back.

- Automatic selection for pickaxes, axes, shovels, hoes, shears and melee weapons, including the blocks other switchers miss: sculk, sponge, hay, bamboo, muddy mangrove roots, nether stems, copper, cobwebs, wool and leaves.
- Fortune and Silk Touch treated as choices about the drop. A tool you deliberately picked up is never swapped away; Smart mode instead picks the enchantment each block deserves.
- Four selection strategies, three durability policies, and a safety floor that skips tools close to breaking.
- Area tools such as Hammers are never auto-selected unless you switch them on.
- Mark any individual tool Preferred or Never auto-select, and register custom tools from other add-ons.
- Sneak to pause. Builder protection and Combat Lock keep it out of your way.
- Per-player settings, operator world settings, and full dedicated-server console support.

Rename a stick to `SwapperControl` on an anvil to open your settings, or `AdminSwapperControl` for the world settings. The add-on adds no items or blocks of its own.

Requires Bedrock 1.26.30+. No experiments, no cheats, achievement friendly, Vibrant Visuals ready.

Full guide: https://github.com/CharlesJGantt/Andys-Smart-Tool-Swapper/wiki
